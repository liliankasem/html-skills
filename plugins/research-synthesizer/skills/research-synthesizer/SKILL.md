# Research Synthesizer

Answer a bounded research question while preserving provenance, disagreement, and uncertainty.

## Shared foundation

Read `./foundation/INSTRUCTIONS.md` and `./foundation/references/contract.md`. Adapt `./foundation/assets/artifact.html` with artifact kind `report`. Remove the example editor unless evidence annotation is requested.

## Inputs

Identify the research question, audience, time scope, authorized source types, and desired decision. Use only accessible sources; do not imply an unavailable connector was consulted.

## Workflow

1. Break the question into a small set of answerable subquestions and define inclusion/exclusion criteria.
2. Gather relevant primary sources first. Record source identity, date or revision where available, and what each source supports.
3. Deduplicate derivative reporting. Compare conflicting accounts and distinguish source claims from independently established facts.
4. Build a claim-to-source map. Label assumptions, unsupported claims, unresolved contradictions, and stale evidence.
5. Synthesize the answer and decision implications, noting what additional evidence could change the conclusion.

## HTML output

Lead with the answer and confidence boundaries. Include thematic findings, comparison or timeline when relevant, source-linked evidence, counterevidence, open questions, and methodology/coverage.

Quote sparingly and paraphrase in original language; do not reproduce protected source material wholesale. Do not leak private source content into public artifacts or publish without authorization.

## Acceptance checks

Every material factual claim has a supporting reference. Dates, quantities, and comparisons match the sources. Recommendations are labeled as analysis and do not conceal contradictory evidence. Check source links and apply the foundation contract.

## Example request

"Synthesize these design discussions, code changes, and docs into an HTML report explaining why the architecture changed."
